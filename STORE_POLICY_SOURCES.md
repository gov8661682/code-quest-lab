# Code Quest Lab - Store Policy Sources

Checked: 2026-08-04

This is implementation research, not legal advice or a compliance determination. Re-check all sources immediately before submission.

## Apple

- [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) - current guidance emphasizes testing/stability, complete metadata, review access, removal of placeholder content, age-rating accuracy, and using In-App Purchase for digital unlocks.
- [Design safe and age-appropriate experiences](https://developer.apple.com/kids/) - Kids-category expectations include age-appropriate content, privacy protections, and parental gates for purchases and external links.
- [App Privacy Details](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/) - App Store privacy disclosures cover data collected by the app and integrated third parties; on-device-only processing may be treated differently from data transmitted off-device.
- [Manage app privacy](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/manage-app-privacy) - a privacy policy URL and accurate App Privacy responses are required for App Store distribution.
- [In-App Purchase](https://developer.apple.com/in-app-purchase/) - Apple provides the purchase system for digital goods and unlocks.

## Google Play / Android

- [Google Play Families Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9893335?hl=en) - child-targeted apps have additional content, data, and monetisation obligations.
- [Target audience and content](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9893335?hl=en) - target audience declarations affect which Families requirements apply.
- [Data safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en) - published data-safety answers must accurately describe collection, sharing, security, and deletion practices.
- [Google Play payments](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738?hl=en) - digital content sold in Play-distributed apps generally uses Google Play billing unless an applicable exception applies.
- [Google Play billing system](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing) - one-time products, purchase tokens, entitlement handling, and secure purchase processing are documented here.
- [Meet Google Play's target API level requirement](https://developer.android.com/google/play/requirements/target-sdk) - the current page states that new apps and updates must target Android 16/API 36 starting 2026-08-31; verify again before the native release.
